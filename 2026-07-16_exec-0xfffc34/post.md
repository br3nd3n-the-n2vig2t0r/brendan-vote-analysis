# YES rationale for the July 16 Executive

I intend to vote **YES** on the July 16 Executive.

The main reason is that the technical evidence is strong. The deployed Core spell matches the
reviewed source, the recorded tests passed, and the Spark, Grove, and Osero payload addresses and
codehashes match their reviewed artifacts. The main disclosed actions were implemented as
expected.

I accept the assumptions needed for unavailable evidence: the July 3 Sheet state matched the
later snapshot; the named private Executive-process steps were completed correctly; and the Prime
reviewers were qualifying independent reviewers who approved before payload delivery.

I also accept Spark's accrued-yield adjustment in the buyback calculation and the temporary risk
from the separate Spark/Grove settlement transactions. Both settlement legs completed
successfully, 144 seconds apart.

There are still public-process concerns. Some deadlines were missed, some provenance and
deployment-checklist links were incomplete, and the Chainlog and Safe Harbor discussions did not
show the expected public sequence. These issues should be improved, but I do not consider them
decisive for this vote.

This is a human override of the earlier draft `NO` recommendation. It accepts the remaining
process risk; it does not claim that every Atlas requirement was publicly demonstrated.
